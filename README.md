# 🧠 TruthRush

### Think Fast. Spot the Fake. Master the Truth.

**TruthRush** is a fast-paced educational web game designed to help kids and teenagers develop **critical thinking and misinformation-detection skills** through fun, interactive gameplay.

Players are shown headlines, facts, social media-style posts, and other pieces of information and must quickly decide:

> **REAL or FAKE?**

Instead of learning about misinformation through traditional lessons, TruthRush turns it into a competitive game with **timers, points, streaks, challenges, and instant explanations**.

## ✨ Features

* 🧠 **Real vs Fake Challenges** — Identify whether information is genuine or misleading.
* ⚡ **Fast-Paced Gameplay** — Make decisions before the timer runs out.
* 🔥 **Streak System** — Build consecutive correct answers for higher scores.
* 🏆 **Score Tracking** — Compete against your previous best.
* 📚 **Instant Explanations** — Learn why an answer is correct or incorrect.
* 🎯 **Progressive Difficulty** — Questions become more challenging as you progress.
* 📱 **Responsive Design** — Playable across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
* 🎨 **Interactive UI** — Designed to feel like a game rather than a traditional educational tool.

## 💡 Inspiration

Kids and teenagers spend a huge part of their day online, surrounded by headlines, social media posts, images, and videos. But how do they know what's **real and what's fake**?

We wanted to turn this important lesson into something fun rather than another boring lecture.

That's how **TruthRush** was born.

Players become **digital truth detectives**, quickly deciding whether information is real or fake. Correct answers earn points and streaks, while incorrect answers provide simple explanations.

Our goal is simple:

> **Teach young people to question what they see, think critically, and spot misinformation — while having fun.**

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Start the game.
2. Read the displayed statement, headline, or content.
3. Decide whether it is **REAL** or **FAKE**.
4. Answer before the timer runs out.
5. Earn points for correct answers.
6. Build your streak to increase your score.
7. Learn from the explanation after each answer.
8. Try again and beat your high score!

---

## 🎯 Gameplay

TruthRush combines **speed, knowledge, and critical thinking**.

### Scoring

Players earn points for correct answers, with additional rewards for maintaining a streak and answering quickly.

Example:

```text
Correct Answer     → +10 points
Fast Answer        → Bonus points
Streak              → Score multiplier
Wrong Answer       → Lose a life
```

The objective is to achieve the highest possible score while maintaining accuracy.

---

## 🧠 Educational Purpose

TruthRush isn't designed to tell players what to believe.

Instead, it encourages them to develop the habit of asking:

* Who created this information?
* Does the claim make sense?
* Is there evidence?
* Could this image or headline be misleading?
* Should I verify this before sharing it?

The game aims to make **critical thinking a habit rather than a homework assignment.**

## 🔐 Responsible AI & Information

TruthRush is intended as an **educational game**, not a replacement for professional fact-checking or reliable news sources.

As AI-powered features are introduced, generated content should be reviewed and verified before being presented as factual information.

The goal is to teach players **how to think critically**, not simply to trust an automated system.
